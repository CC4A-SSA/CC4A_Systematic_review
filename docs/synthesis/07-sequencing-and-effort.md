## 3. Working model: AI does the volume, people do the judgement

The synthesis is run AI-first. That is what makes the scope feasible at Namita's time allocation, and it is why the verification protocol (`04`) is as strict as it is.

| Step | AI | People |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery and search | Runs and logs searches, citation chasing, ERA queries | Namita sets strings and checks recall at calibration |
| Screening | Two independent screening passes | Namita resolves disagreements and checks the exclude sample |
| Extraction | Fills the template with quotes and locators, including site-seasons | Namita spot-checks and signs off each batch |
| Verification | A separate session or model runs checks A–C | Adjudicator resolves failures; Pete or E4 audits 10 % end to end |
| Estimation | Runs the pre-specified model code | Namita and S5 check fits and sensitivity runs |
| Recipe drafting | Drafts recipe text and fields from the verified tables | Namita proposes tiers and routing; the review gate signs off |

Where people's time goes is the calibration at the start of each practice, adjudication, and the judgements that cannot be delegated: mechanism, routing, tier and the gap statements.

Other ways to protect the dates, if the pilots show they are tight:

- **Consume Adaptation Insights** wherever their synthesis covers a practice (`08`).
- **Breadth first** for the ten non-pilot recipes: definition, mechanism and production effect for all of them; a buffering coefficient only where stress-season data turn up in the time box, otherwise route D for adaptation, stated plainly.
- **Pete sets the bronze loss-matrix values**, with Namita supplying the literature anchors.
- **Co-own Stream 4 with the Activity 4 team** (`00` Q2).

## 4. Handover in mid-December

Before Namita's last day, one short status note in this folder (`STATUS - mid-December handover.md`):

- per practice: stage reached (not started / searched / extracted / verified / estimated / reviewed), the time box used, and whether the search reached citation closure;
- open verification failures and disputes;
- what S5, the reviewers, Adaptation Insights and Chun still owe, and what was agreed with them;
- protocol amendments made since the freeze;
- anything she would do differently for the remaining practices.

The priority order up to then is what makes the handover clean: pilots through the gate, the full loss matrix, and the Stream 2 register first; the remaining recipes breadth first so that each is at a usable stage rather than a few finished and the rest untouched.

## 5. What the pilots must prove by mid-November

Not just three recipes. The pilots are where the machinery is tested:

- the extraction template works for crop (biochar), irrigated (AWD, water-supply method) and livestock (rotational grazing) cases;
- S5's season classification round-trip works and has a turnaround time;
- the verification pass catches something (if it catches nothing in three recipes, suspect the check, not the extraction);
- the review gate has named reviewers and a turnaround time.

Set the time boxes for the remaining recipes from what the pilots actually took.

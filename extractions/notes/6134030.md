Extractor: Claude subagent (claude-opus-5-5), 2026-09-30. Build script: scratchpad/build_6134030.py; self-check: scratchpad/check_6134030.py.

## Arms (p. 3, §2.2)
- **NT**: no soil disturbance (disk-opener slots), surface cover of "crop residues and mulches (8 Mg ha-1)", glyphosate weed control.
- **SM** (stubble-mulching): ploughed to 15 cm (the same as CT), then mulch 8 Mg ha-1 + previous-season residues, glyphosate.
- **DT**: ploughed to 0–40 cm, no cover, hand-hoe weeding.
- **CT**: ploughed to 0–15 cm, no cover, hand-hoe weeding.
- All arms: cultivar NABE 4, 90 kg ha-1 seed, no fertiliser. CRD design, 2 replicates per site, 2 sites × 3 seasons (Apr 2019–Jun 2020).

## Judgement calls
1. **Practice = ca_bundle for both studies.** The paper has no other in-scope practice.
2. **STU-01 NT vs CT = `defined_bundle`.** It has minimum disturbance (b38) plus surface residue cover (b27) against conventional tillage with no cover. Caveats:
   - The weed control differs: herbicide in NT, hand hoe in CT. 01 §2.4 lists weed-control method as a CA dose descriptor, so I did not treat this as confounding. I set rob_comparator_fidelity = some.
   - The cover is "crop residues and mulches" at 8 Mg ha-1. The paper does not say where the mulch came from, so it may be imported material rather than retained residue. Flag this for the reviewer.
   - The NT plot history ("previously used for maize… two years fallow") is stated only in the NT bullet. It is unclear whether the other plots share it, so I set rob_confounding = some.
3. **STU-02 SM vs CT = `partial_bundle`.** SM is ploughed to 15 cm, the same as CT, so there is no tillage reduction. The contrast is mulch plus herbicide on tilled soil, which is the residue component only (the "mulch without a tillage change" case). era_codes = b27; h6.
4. **DT not extracted.** Deep tillage is neither the practice nor the CA comparator (h6). It is logged in source.note. NT-vs-DT contrasts are not extracted.
5. **One trial_id (TRL-6134030).** Both sites belong to one designed experiment, reported together. The site_season rows have study_id = STU-01 but are shared by STU-02 effect rows (the same physical plots and seasons). Duplicating them would double-count site-seasons. CT is a shared control: see `shared_control_group` (CT-<site>-<season>, CT-pooled_all).
6. **management = on_station, design = experiment_on_station.** The paper calls them "experimental sites" run with a tractor and never mentions farmers. They could be on-farm researcher-managed; this is uncertain.
7. **Site-season grain yields come from Figure 6 (`figure_digitised`).** The text gives only pooled means. The bars are vector graphics, so I read the heights from the PDF drawing coordinates, calibrated on the axis ticks. Values come out on round numbers (1850, 1010, 500 …). Reading 2 was a visual check on a render.
   - **Cross-check:** the six-value means reproduce the printed pooled means exactly: NT 1842.5, SM 1794.5, CT 1010.0, DT 1083.3.
   - The WUE × ET values from Table 2 also agree approximately (e.g. Kimenyedde CT 2019-2: 0.28 × 178.11 × 10 ≈ 499 against the digitised 500).
8. **SEs from the Figure 6 error bars** are not put in the effect rows, because there is one dispersion field and two arms. They are recorded here instead (kg ha-1, SE):

   | Site–season | NT | SM | CT |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Goma 2019-1 | 62.5 | 79.5 | 40.4 |
   | Goma 2019-2 | 82.0 | 79.5 | 50.0 |
   | Goma 2020 | 82.5 | 82.0 | 54.9 |
   | Kim 2019-1 | 82.3 | 81.6 | 34.9 |
   | Kim 2019-2 | 81.6 | 78.2 | 15.2 |
   | Kim 2020 | 82.3 | 77.5 | 47.4 |

   Many NT/SM SEs are about 4.5% of the mean, which looks formulaic.
9. **ET and WUE rows.** ET per site-season (Table 2) is entered as outcome = water_use (mm, growing-season actual ET from the soil water balance). WUE-grain is outcome = other (kg m-3). The table has no dispersion. n = 2 plots per arm per site.
10. **SWS rows.** Soil water storage at planting and at harvest (0–100 cm) is entered as soil_moisture, pooled_all. It is mechanism evidence. The paper does not say explicitly what these means are averaged over; I assumed seasons and sites.
11. **n left blank on pooled rows.** The number of plot-seasons pooled is not stated.
12. **author_condition.**
    - Both 2019 2nd seasons are `stress`: rainfall is below the long-term mean (Goma 397 vs 486 mm; Kimenyedde 392 mm, the lowest of the three seasons), and the authors attribute low yields to a 12-day dry spell during grain filling.
    - The other seasons are `normal`: rainfall is above or close to the long-term mean.
    - Ambiguity: §4.3 also says "dry spells (about ten days at flowering) were common during the growing seasons", without naming seasons.
    - Note that CT at Goma yielded *more* in 2019-2 (1200) than in 2019-1 (1010). The stress signal is strong only at Kimenyedde (CT 500).
13. **Coordinates.** The PDF renders 00°25′0″N 32°42′0″E (Goma) and 00°32′0″N 32°50′0″E (Kimenyedde). The text layer garbles the degree glyphs. Converted to decimal: 0.4167/32.7 and 0.5333/32.8333, with coord_source = reported. Only minute precision is given, so accuracy is about 1.8 km.
14. **Planting and harvest dates** are read by eye from the Figure 3 calendar image. Sowing date = planting_date (land preparation came 2–3 weeks earlier). Season-2 harvests fall in Nov 2019, so year = 2019.
15. **Site soil values** are the initial site values from §2.1 (pH, OC, texture), not the per-treatment values in Table 1. Initial OC (5.2%, 4.9%) is inconsistent with the Table 1 0–20 cm values (≤4.54%). I took the §2.1 values as printed.
16. **years_since_establishment = 0.** The trial started April 2019 and the observations span conversion years 0–1. Expect early-year CA effects.

## Costs
- These are the Table 3 "Gross cost (USD ha-1)" rows for NT, SM and CT over 14 months (three seasons). I set cost_class = total and author_computed = yes.
- unit_basis = per_ha, because there is no enum value for "per ha per 14 months / 3 seasons". The period is stated in `item`.
- Boundary (QTE-030, §2.7): "land rent, cost for tillage, machinery, seeds, mulches, herbicide, insecticide, labor, harvesting, etc." The boundary is generic, not itemised per arm. From it:
  - labour_included = yes and inputs_included = yes.
  - opportunity_cost_included = yes, on the basis of land rent. A reviewer may prefer `unstated`.
  - equipment_included = unstated: machinery cost is included, but not whether it was hired, purchased or depreciated.
  - family_labour_costed, transport, training and certification are unstated.
- The currency is USD as reported. The authors converted from UGX at an unstated rate; the price is quoted as Ugshs 3800 = USD 1.03/kg.
- price_year = 2020 (assumed_data_year; the data span 2019–2020).
- DT cost not extracted.
- Not extracted, per the rules: gross revenue, net profit, BCR, bean price.

## Inconsistencies in the paper
- The abstract says NT yield is 5/38/43% above SM/DT/CT; §3.4 says 3/41/45%. The Figure 6 values support §3.4.
- The 2019 2nd-season mean yield is 1335 kg ha-1 in §3.4 but 1295 in §4.3.
- In Table 2 the ANOVA "Season" effect on ET has F = 129.84 yet is listed as "ns". The F and p values look unreliable.

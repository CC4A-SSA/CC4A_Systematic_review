# Extraction notes: StudyID 6135863 (Tufa et al. 2022, Adv. Agric., doi 10.1155/2022/5400431)

Extractor: Claude subagent (claude-opus-5-5), 2026-09-30. Files built by a script from the page-tagged text dump; every verbatim was sliced from the dump and then re-checked (31/31 found after whitespace collapse).

## Practice assignment
- `practice_id = biochar`. This is a **field** trial ("a ﬁeld experiment was conducted in 2020", p. 1; 15.75 m2 plots, oxen ploughing, p. 2), not a pot trial. Feedstock is maize cob, made in a pit (p. 2, Section 2.5). Pit char is in scope under 01 §2.2.
- The char is applied on its own, not blended with compost and not a biochar-based fertiliser. NPS is a separate factor.

## Contrast type
- `contrast_type = isolated`. The 5 × 3 factorial (biochar 0/2/4/6/8 t/ha × NPS 0/50/100 kg/ha, RCBD, 3 reps) gives 12 contrasts in which only the biochar rate differs: each biochar rate against 0 t/ha biochar **at the same NPS rate**. All plots got 200 kg/ha urea, and the other practices were "applied uniformly for all treatments" (p. 2). The comparator is therefore "same NPS rate + urea, no biochar". I did not extract contrasts that mix NPS rates (e.g. 8 t + 100 NPS vs the unfertilised T1, which is the basis of the authors' "+16%/+18%" headline). Those are confounded.
- Even the "0 kg NPS" arm is fertilised: it gets 200 kg/ha urea (about 92 kg N/ha). So `era_codes = b28; h10` and `comparator_era_code = h10` (fertilised control), not h10.1.

## Arms and outcomes included
- **Grain yield (EFF-001..012)**: all 12 biochar-vs-no-biochar contrasts. None dropped. Every contrast is positive in the point estimate (NPS 0: 6.22–6.30 vs 5.96; NPS 50: 6.32–6.96 vs 6.02; NPS 100: 6.91–7.03 vs 6.46 t/ha). Values are the **unadjusted** "Grain yield (t·ha−1)" column of Table 7 (p. 8). I did not use the 10%-deducted "Adjusted grain yield". Figure 1 plots the same means. Its bar heights match Table 7 on inspection, but no numbers are printed on it, so nothing was digitised. The `shared_control_group` values are CTRL-T1-NPS0, CTRL-T2-NPS50 and CTRL-T3-NPS100.
- **Above-ground biological yield (EFF-013..016)**: biochar **main-effect** means from Table 6 (averaged over the three NPS rates, so still isolated for biochar). There is no interaction table for biomass. n = 9 per mean (3 NPS rates × 3 reps). I computed this and recorded it in the computation field of QTE-014. `aggregation = site_season` because there is only one site-season. The pooling is across fertiliser levels, not across seasons.
- Not extracted (yield components or growth, which fall outside the outcome priorities): ear weight and thousand-kernel weight (Table 5), kernels per ear and harvest index (Table 6), plant height and LAI (Table 4), phenology (Table 3). Net benefit, gross benefit, MRR and prices were not extracted, per the rules.
- `reported_condition = unknown` and `author_condition = unknown`: the authors never describe the 2020 season as normal or stressed.

## Dispersion
- Grain yield: `dispersion_type = lsd`, `0.227` (Figure 1 legend, "LSD (0.05) = 0.227", CV 12.1%). **Flag:** these two are internally inconsistent. With a mean of about 6.5 t/ha, CV 12.1% and 3 reps, the LSD should be about 1.3 t/ha. One of the two printed statistics is probably wrong. The LSD applies to the Figure 1 interaction means, and I assumed those are the Table 7 values.
- Biomass: LSD 0.938 (Table 6, biochar main-effect LSD).

## Discrepancies in the paper
- The unfertilised control's grain yield is 5.975 t/ha in the text (p. 7) but 5.96 in Table 7. I used Table 7 throughout, for consistency across arms. The text value is quoted in QTE-031.
- The NPS price is 15.50 ETB/kg in Section 2.8 (p. 4) but 15 ETB/kg in the Table 7 footnote. Neither matches the TVCs: 50 kg NPS gives TVC 712.50, i.e. 14.25 ETB/kg including application. The biochar TVC increments are 50 ETB per 2 t/ha, which is 0.025 ETB/kg, not the stated 0.25 ETB/kg application cost. I recorded the TVC values exactly as printed.
- The Table 7 footnote could not be quoted cleanly: the "=" signs are an undecodable control character (\x88) in the text dump. The boundary quote is the Section 2.8 sentence instead (QTE-015).

## Costs (CST-001..015)
- One row per arm (T1–T15), each the authors' "TVC (ETB ha−1)" from Table 7. `cost_class = total` and `author_computed = yes`, because TVC is the authors' aggregate of variable costs. It could arguably be classed `variable_running`. `unit_basis = per_ha` as printed (single season). Currency is ETB, not converted.
- Boundary (QTE-015): TVC contains only the NPS fertiliser price and an "application cost of biochar and NPS" of 0.25 ETB/kg. The **biochar material or production cost (feedstock, pit, labour to make it) appears not to be priced**. Urea, common to all plots, is excluded. So `inputs_included = yes` (NPS only). `labour_included = unstated`: the "application cost" is probably labour but is not stated as such. Family labour, transport, equipment, training, MRV and opportunity cost are all `unstated`. `cost_bearer = unstated`.
- `price_year = 2020` with `price_year_basis = assumed_data_year`. Input prices carry no year. Only the maize output price is dated ("December 2020", p. 4), and output prices are not extracted.
- `cost_basis_source = trial_record`: these are researcher partial-budget costs for the trial, not farmer recall.

## Other judgement calls
- `management = on_farm_researcher_managed` / `design = experiment_on_farm_researcher`: **uncertain**. The paper says "experimental site ... in Guto Gida district" and never says whether this is a research station or a farmer's field. It is clearly researcher-managed.
- Coordinates: reported as "(09o 11′N–37o 09′E)". Converted from degrees-minutes to 9.1833, 37.15 (computation recorded in QTE-004). `coord_source = reported`. This is a single point for the district-level site.
- `water_regime = rainfed`. It is inferred: the paper describes rainfall and the rainy season and never mentions irrigation.
- `years_since_establishment = 0`: the char was applied once, one week before planting, in the single observed season.
- `dose_farm_feasible = unclear` at study level. 2 t/ha is plausibly feasible from maize cobs. 8 t/ha is probably not: the paper claims about 50% char yield from cob (p. 2), which still implies about 16 t cob/ha. `meets_methodology_definition = unclear`.
- RoB: assignment `low` (RCBD with 3 reps, though the randomisation procedure is not described). Selective reporting `low` (all 15 arms reported, single season). Outcome measurement `low` (harvested and weighed from the net plot).
- `site_season.soil_organic_carbon_pct` left blank: the paper gives OC as 3.4 g/kg (= 0.34%), and I did not convert it. Note that 3.4 g/kg OC looks implausibly low alongside 1.12 g/kg total N (C:N about 3). It may be a units error in the paper.
- The province (admin1) is not stated in the paper and was left blank.

## Not found in the paper
- Planting date and harvest date (only "one week prior to planting" and "40 days after planting" relative timings).
- Seasonal rainfall for 2020. Only the long-term annual range of 1200–1450 mm is given, not recorded as seasonal_rainfall_mm.
- Pyrolysis temperature and char yield of the pit process.
- Any SD/SE per treatment. There is only one LSD and CV per variable.
- Labour days, and any biochar production cost.
- AEZ. Altitude is 1450–1700 m a.s.l.
- Randomisation details; whether the site is on-station or on-farm.

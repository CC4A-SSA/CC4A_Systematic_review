Every verbatim is sliced programmatically from the whitespace-collapsed text dump, so the dump's control characters (for example `ha\x011` for ha⁻¹, `\x03` for the degree sign) and the `¼` (=) and `þ` (+) glyphs are kept.

## Trial and design
- One physical trial: `TRL-6135671`. It was a single on-farm experiment, 2017/2018 cropping season, at Mosobo Kebele, Yilmana Densa, West Gojam, Ethiopia. Design: RCBD, 3 reps, 3x3 factorial of urea/NPSB (0/0, 50/50, 100/100 kg/ha) x compost (0, 5, 10 t/ha, dry-weight basis). Maize variety BH-540 in every arm.
- management = on_farm_researcher_managed; design = experiment_on_farm_researcher. The paper says "farmers' fields" and gives plot-level RCBD with researcher-defined treatments. n_sites = 1, n_seasons = 1, n_site_seasons = 1. The paper does not say whether the replicates were on different farms.
- The trial is split into four study rows because comparator and contrast_type differ by contrast. All four share one trial_id.
- One site_season row per study (SS-01..04). **All four are the same physical site-season**, duplicated only so each effect's site_season has the same parent study. S5 should classify it once. Do not count it as four site-seasons.

## Practice assignment and scenario (b) scope
All rows use practice_id = nutrient_mgmt_b.

**Comparator caveat (applies to STU-01 and STU-02).** 01 §2.6 wants farmer or recommended fertiliser practice as the comparator. The paper's 100/100 kg/ha urea/NPSB arm is the "full dose"/"highest rate of mineral fertilizer". It is **not** stated to be the extension recommendation: the paper gives that as 150 kg DAP + 100 kg urea/ha (a different product). It is also not farmer practice, which the paper gives as a national average of 45 kg DAP + 33 kg urea/ha (QTE-015). The 50/50 arm is nearer the farmer rate, but again this is not stated. The code h10 is used for both sole-mineral comparators, and rob_comparator_fidelity = some for STU-02.

**Not extracted as effects:**
- Sole mineral (50/50, 100/100) vs 0/0. This is a plain rate increase against zero, which 01 §2.6 excludes explicitly. The arm means still appear in the data as the STU-01/02 control means.
- Sole compost vs sole mineral (full substitution). This is not organic + inorganic.
- Biomass for STU-03/04, TGW, straw yield, harvest index.
- Post-harvest soil properties (Table 3).

## Effect details
- Grain yield (GY) and above-ground dry biomass (AGDB) come from Table 4 (p. 5), which I checked against the rendered page image. n = 3 per arm (RCBD, 3 reps). Dispersion is the LSD(0.05) printed in Table 4: GY 0.25, AGDB 0.86. Unit t/ha; grain adjusted to 12.5% moisture.
- aggregation = site_season; reported_condition = unknown. The authors give no rainfall or condition for the trial season; only a 2004–2017 long-term mean annual rainfall of 1421.3 mm, which is not recorded as seasonal rainfall.
- Shared control groups:
  - CG-0+50/50 (EFF-001..004).
  - CG-0+100/100 (EFF-005..008 and STU-02 EFF-009..012). The same control arm is used across two studies, so do not count it twice when pooling.
  - CG-0/0 (EFF-013..018).
- The Table 4 header prints "Compost (kg ha-1)". The methods and Table 5 give t ha-1, so I treat this as a typo and use 0/5/10 t/ha.
- Section 3.2 text says the highest GY is "6.06 t ha-1", while Table 4 prints 6.07. I used the table value.

## Site-season
- Coordinates are the midpoint of the bounding box reported in section 2.1: 11°15'49"–11°19'35" N, 37°26'47"–37°29'21" E, giving lat 11.2950, lon 37.4678. The computation is in QTE-009. coord_source = reported (derived from reported bounds, not geocoded). The exact plot location within the box is not given.
- year = 2017. The paper says only "2017/2018 cropping season", and only in the Abstract; the body does not restate the year. In Ethiopian usage this normally means the 2017 main (meher) season, which the paper places in June–September. Composting ended in early 2017 and compost went on 21 days before sowing, which fits 2017 planting. **This is still an inference.**
- Planting and harvest dates are not stated, so they are blank; S5 should use the crop calendar.
- author_condition = unknown.
- Soil values are from Table 1 (at planting): pH(H2O) 5.54, OC 1.29%, clay 42%, texture "Clay". Section 2.4 text says "clay loam", which is a source inconsistency.
- aez_reported: "tepid moist to cool highlands".

## Study-field judgements
- water_regime = rainfed. This is **inferred**: no irrigation is mentioned, and the study area has a June–September rainy season (QTE-012).
- dose_farm_feasible = unclear. The authors themselves say smallholders cannot afford the required amounts of compost (QTE-014). 5–10 t/ha dry-weight compost is a large amount.
- meets_methodology_definition = unclear: scenario (b) is not yet closed with TNC.
- rob_assignment = low (randomised RCBD). rob_confounding = low (same field). rob_outcome_measurement = low (harvested and weighed). rob_selective_reporting = low (all 9 arms reported, single season).
- years_since_establishment is left blank. It is not required for nutrient management, and the paper does not say whether the field had compost history. Compost was applied in the trial season.
- era_codes:
  - compost is b29; comparators are h10 and h10.1 (from 01 §2.6).
  - I did not assign a leaf code to urea/NPSB. The vocabulary available to me does not say which of b17/b21/b16/b23 is N, P or blend, so I wrote "not resolved: nearest b17/b21/b16/b23" rather than inventing one.

## Costs (Table 5, p. 6)
- There are 20 rows: 8 non-control arms, each with CC (cost of compost) where compost > 0, CIF (cost of inorganic fertilizer) where mineral > 0, and TC (total cost).
  - Zero-valued cells were not written as rows.
  - The 0/0 control has TC = 0.
- CC = variable_running, labour_included = yes, cost_basis_source = expert_estimate. Section 2.7 says compost cost is "labour cost of compost preparation based on World Food Program work norms". It is a normed figure, not recorded labour.
- CIF = variable_running, inputs_included = yes, cost_basis_source = trial_record (trial quantities × current prices).
- TC = total with author_computed = yes. TC = CC + CIF is visible in the table; I computed nothing.
- Boundary quote for every row: QTE-018 (section 2.7). It lists only urea and NPSB prices and compost-preparation labour. Excluded items are therefore **implied, not stated**:
  - Fertiliser application labour, compost material or opportunity cost, transport, equipment and training are all left `unstated`.
  - A stricter reading would set them to `no`.
- family_labour_costed = unstated; the WFP norm does not say whether the labour is family or hired.
- currency ETB, unit_basis per_ha (as printed, "Birr ha-1", single season).
- price_year = 2017 with price_year_basis = assumed_data_year; the paper says only "current prices".
- No labour person-days are reported.
- Not extracted: prices (urea 12.9 and NPSB 15.2 Birr/kg, maize 6.5 Birr/kg, exchange rate), gross benefit, net benefit, MRR.

You are screening titles and abstracts for an evidence review. The review (CC4A, Carbon Credits for Adaptation) collects numbers on the costs, adoption and carbon income of farming practices in Africa, to inform carbon finance for adaptation.

Answer every question from the title and abstract only. Do not use outside knowledge about the paper. When the title and abstract do not let you tell, answer "unclear" rather than guess: unclear records go to a person, so an honest "unclear" is better than a wrong "no". The review would rather keep a doubtful paper than lose a relevant one.

## The questions

1. **countries**: the countries where the study was done, as named in the title or abstract. Empty if none are named.

2. **in_africa**: "yes" if the study was done in one or more African countries, "no" if it was done only outside Africa, "unclear" if no location is given.

3. **practices**: the codes of the practices, from the list below, that the study tests, evaluates or surveys. A practice only mentioned in passing does not count. Empty if none.

4. **practice_tested**: "yes" if the study produces evidence on a listed practice (a field trial, an on-farm comparison, a farm or household survey of users, a project evaluation, an economic analysis of the practice); "no" if the listed practices are only background or not studied; "unclear" if you cannot tell.

5. **reports_cost**: "yes" if the study reports or analyses costs of the practice: establishment, input, labour or variable costs, labour requirements, profitability, gross margins, net returns, cost-benefit analysis, net present value, internal rate of return, benefit-cost ratio, payback. "no" if it does not. "unclear" if you cannot tell.

6. **reports_adoption**: "yes" if the study reports adoption, uptake, extent or use of the practice by farmers, dis-adoption, or what drives adoption (determinants, willingness to pay or accept, choice experiments, payment experiments). "no" or "unclear" otherwise.

7. **reports_carbon_income**: "yes" if the study reports carbon credits, carbon or ecosystem-service payments, carbon revenue, or how income from them is shared with farmers. "no" or "unclear" otherwise.

8. **study_type**: one of
   - "field_experiment": a field or on-farm trial with treatments
   - "farm_survey": a household, farm or plot survey, including adoption studies
   - "project_evaluation": an evaluation of a programme or project
   - "economic_analysis": a cost-benefit, budget or financial analysis built on primary data
   - "modelling_only": a simulation, crop model or scenario study with no new field or survey data
   - "review_or_meta_analysis": a review, synthesis, systematic review or meta-analysis
   - "other": anything else (lab or pot study, method paper, opinion)

9. **evidence_quote**: a short phrase copied word for word from the abstract that shows the cost, adoption or carbon income evidence. Empty if there is none.

10. **reason**: one plain sentence explaining your answers.

11. **verdict**: your own overall call, "include" if the study is in Africa, studies a listed practice and reports cost, adoption or carbon income evidence; "exclude" if it clearly fails one of these; "unsure" otherwise.
